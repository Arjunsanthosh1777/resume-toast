# Sample Job Description: Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer

**Company**: NexusFlow Technologies  
**Position**: Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer (Product Infrastructure)  
**Location**: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid / Remote Option)  
**Compensation**: $160,000 - $190,000 + Equity + Comprehensive Benefits  
**Experience Level**: Senior (4-7+ years)

---

### About NexusFlow
NexusFlow is an enterprise SaaS platform empowering over 2,500 mid-market and enterprise organizations to orchestrate cross-departmental operations in real time. We process over 80 million events daily and are backed by top-tier Tier-1 venture capital. We are expanding our core product engineering team to build the next generation of our analytics and workflow automation engine.

---

### The Role & What You Will Do
We are looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer who thrives on building scalable web applications and high-throughput backend services. You will lead the architecture of customer-facing dashboards, design resilient APIs, and mentor junior and mid-level engineers.

#### Key Responsibilities:
- Architect, build, and maintain high-performance web applications using **React, TypeScript, Node.js**, and modern cloud infrastructure.
- Lead end-to-end feature development from technical design documents (RFCs) through to production deployment and monitoring.
- Optimize database schemas and queries across **PostgreSQL and Redis** to handle high-concurrency workloads with sub-200ms latency SLAs.
- Own CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation using **Docker, Kubernetes**, and **AWS (ECS, S3, RDS, CloudWatch)**.
- Drive code quality, technical architecture reviews, and automated testing across unit, integration, and end-to-end suites.
- Collaborate with Product Management, UX Design, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) to scope features and resolve customer bottlenecks.
- Mentor junior engineers through structured 1-on-1s, pair programming, and rigorous code reviews.

---

### Minimum Qualifications:
- **4+ years of professional full-stack software development experience** in production environments.
- Deep expertise in modern **JavaScript/TypeScript**, **React**, and **Node.js** ecosystems.
- Demonstrated experience designing, querying, and optimizing relational databases (**PostgreSQL** preferred).
- Hands-on experience containerizing applications using **Docker** and deploying to cloud infrastructure (**AWS** or GCP).
- Proven track record of improving application performance, latency, or reliability with quantifiable metrics.
- Experience writing comprehensive automated tests (**Jest, React Testing Library, Cypress**, or Playwright).
- Strong communication skills and ability to lead architectural discussions.

---

### Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience migrating legacy monolithic architectures to microservices or event-driven patterns.
- Familiarity with distributed caching architectures (**Redis**, Memcached).
- Experience handling real-time data streaming or websockets (**Socket.io, Apache Kafka**).
- Prior experience working in high-growth B2B SaaS environments handling millions of active records.
- Track record of active contribution to engineering blogs, open-source projects, or tech community events.
